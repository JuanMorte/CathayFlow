# Decisions

Format: date · decision · why · who.

- 2026-11-14 · No roles: one person owns all the code, with one coding agent (`AGENTS.md` §6, `CLAUDE.md`) · the five-role split was written for a team; a single owner makes it dead weight · Alex.
- 2026-11-14 · The prepared instructions (`AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/`) and the team's own Figma design are the only pre-made material in the repo; no pre-collected data is in it · confirmed as fine under the hackathon rules · Alex.
- 2026-11-14 · Commits stay with Alex; the agent does not commit · Alex's call · Alex.
- 2026-11-14 · The recorder Lambda is named `<prefix>-hkia-recorder` (default `flow-hkia-recorder`) rather than the bare `hkia-recorder` in AGENTS.md §8.2 · two deployments in one account must not collide, and every other resource already carries the prefix · agent, to be confirmed by Alex.
- 2026-11-14 · "CX-operated" means the first entry of HKIA's `flight` list has carrier CX; codeshares on other airlines are not Cathay connections · the first entry is the operating flight in the real data · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · For a multi-stop flight the parser's `airport` is the first origin for arrivals and the final destination for departures; all stops stay in `airports` · matches the order in the real data · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Deploy straight to the hackathon-provided AWS account, profile `flow-hackathon`, region `ap-southeast-1` (the organisers' sign-in runs in `ap-east-1`; any deploy region is allowed) · skips the account move in AGENTS.md §8.9 with under a day left · Alex.
- 2026-11-14 · Every make target that touches AWS refuses to run without `AWS_PROFILE` · without it the AWS CLI silently used another project's default credentials on this laptop · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Hackathon account rules change the infrastructure (AGENTS.md §8.1.4, §8.2, §17.3.10): no `cdk bootstrap` (stacks deploy with the caller's credentials; `flow-data` creates the staging bucket and image repository and is deployed with plain CloudFormation), managed policies instead of inline, roles and policies retained, budget alert dropped · the permission set denies `iam:PutRolePolicy`, role and policy deletion and edits, and `budgets:*`; a failed bootstrap would have left a stack we could not clean up · Alex approved.
- 2026-11-14 · The product has no chat assistant and no language model; the idea is dropped and removed from the spec and the code · Alex's call · Alex.
- 2026-11-14 · The app's two roles live in `flow-data`, not `flow-app` · the account cannot delete roles, and `flow-app` is destroyed and redeployed often; each redeploy would leave another pair behind · agent, approved by Alex.
- 2026-11-14 · The app's task role carries spare permissions from the first deploy: delete under `state/` only, invoke the recorder Lambda, ECS Exec channels, and CloudWatch metrics in the `Flow` namespace · its policy cannot be edited later, so likely needs go in now; each is limited to this project's resources, and nothing under `raw/` can be deleted · Alex asked for headroom; agent chose the set.
- 2026-11-14 · The CDK entry file is `infra/cdk_app.py`, not `app.py` · `app` is the backend package, and the old name shadowed it in tests · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · The image installs exact versions from `backend/requirements.lock`, exported from `uv.lock` by `make lock-backend`, and copies `backend/app` instead of installing it · AGENTS.md §8.5's `pip install ./flowcore ./backend` would ignore the lock (§17.2.1) · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Connecting passengers per arrival = seats (OAG aircraft type × published Cathay layout) × 87.2% Cathay load factor (Aug 2026) × 28% HKG transfer share (AAHK, Oct 2025); cabins follow the aircraft · the old per-pair random count (mean 2.21) gave every flight 18–54 regardless of size and could not be argued on stage · Alex asked for defensible proportions.
- 2026-11-14 · Free seats on later flights = seats per cabin × (1 − load factor); the 0.5 seats/hour depletion is dropped · one published figure explains both how full flights are and what is spare; depletion would have emptied Business cabins on a replay that starts at 05:30 · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Replay starts at 05:30 HKT, so CX254's late pushback (published 05:37) turns Mei from Safe (15%) to At risk (67%) on screen · the delay was otherwise published long before the old 16:00 start · Alex.
- 2026-11-14 · Missed with no seat held → `needs_manual_rebooking` for staff · 44 passengers in the scenario run hit this; leaving them silently "missed" was not defensible · Alex agreed.
- 2026-11-14 · Drafts go to staff from a 40% chance of missing (`tau_offer`), not only above 70% · otherwise at-risk passengers like Mei (56%) are never offered a switch before landing · agent; Alex reviewed the flow.
- 2026-11-14 · Web app uses React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind and React Router only; no TanStack Query, Framer Motion or shadcn/ui (AGENTS.md §5) · fewer moving parts for one night; plain fetch plus one WebSocket hook covers the screens · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Staff console replay buttons: Start of day, Before landing (90 min before the hero's best-known gate time), At landing, Play/Pause, two speeds · the demo script needs fixed jump points; "Before landing" uses only what is known at the moment it is pressed · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · The hero's row is pinned to the top of the staff table · the demo must not depend on scrolling to find Mei · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Engine calibrated on measured data: in-flight estimate spread 11 min and bias +4 min (OAG vs HKIA on 30 arrivals), and late departures from history move gate close · the first backtest showed the day-before forecast overconfident (47% predicted, 29% observed); after the change predicted and observed agree within a few points · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Break rates on /impact are labelled SIMULATED, not REAL · they combine real timing with an assumed transfer time · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · The evidence builder is `scripts/build_evidence.py`, not in `research/` · the policy replay runs the backend simulation, and research never imports backend (AGENTS.md §6) · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · M4 in reduced form: empirical delay distributions instead of a trained LightGBM model, no `processed/flights.parquet` or `models/` upload · the backtest shows the in-flight estimate, not a delay model, carries the forecast; a model can come later · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · The passenger app shows no chance of missing and no "Plan A / Plan B": one card is the original flight ("Original · still held"), the other the flight if it is missed ("If you miss it"), with no explanatory lines · Alex's call: likelihoods and plan labels read oddly to a traveller · Alex.
- 2026-11-14 · The greeting uses the passenger's first name ("Hello Mei"), not a title · a title would have to be invented, which means guessing gender · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Origin departure and destination arrival on the phone are the REAL scheduled times from OAG (new `origin_departure` / `dest_arrival` on FlightRef), not sample times · the sample London departure (21:55) contradicted the real 22:15 and the 05:37 pushback · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Gate Planner road times: Google Routes for the same weekday and hour on a fixed date (Thu 19 Nov 2026), rounded to the hour; 5 starting points × 11:00–21:00 cached (110 calls) · Google only quotes traffic for the future, and a fixed date keeps the cache valid for the offline demo; any failure falls back to the Airport Express timetable and says so · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Gate Planner plans for a far gate (north-west, the commonest Cathay gates) until the real gate is published (OAG timeline) · a planner should err early; a published gate can only move "leave by" later · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Gate Planner lives under the passenger app's Travel tools tab · it serves passengers departing Hong Kong, not Mei's transfer story · agent.
- 2026-11-14 · Nothing is copied from the trial bucket (no older HKIA days, so no Typhoon Noul days; no trial research results), until told otherwise. M1 backfills the live 91-day HKIA window; M4 computes the evidence fresh, with disruption days taken from inside that window · keeps all data collected during the competition · Alex.
